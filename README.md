### Hi, I'm Andres Ramasco

I'm a PhD candidate in economics at the **University of Notre Dame** and I'm on the **2026/2027 job market**. I work in applied microeconomics, mainly health economics and its intersection with public economics. Most of my research is about preventable causes of death.

**Job market paper:** *Sinaloa Cartel Presence and its Impact on Fentanyl Mortality in the United States.* I use DEA intelligence on cartel control across U.S. field offices in an event-study difference-in-differences design. After 2014, fentanyl mortality rose by 5.5 deaths per 100,000 more in Sinaloa-dominated counties than in counties controlled by rival cartels, about 30% of the overall rise in my sample.

I'm also a Graduate Student Affiliate at the [Kellogg Institute for International Studies](https://kellogg.nd.edu/) and a Junior Researcher at [REDNIE](https://www.rednie.com.ar/).
